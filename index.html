Earthquake Safety

Essential steps to protect yourself during and after an earthquake

1

Drop, Cover, and Hold On

Immediately drop to your hands and knees, take cover under a sturdy desk or table, and hold on to it. If no table is available, cover your head and neck with your arms.

2

Stay Where You Are

If indoors, stay indoors. If outdoors, stay outdoors. Do not run outside during shaking as most injuries occur when people try to move during an earthquake.

3

Avoid Dangerous Areas

Stay away from windows, mirrors, hanging objects, tall furniture, and exterior walls that could fall or shatter.

4

If in a Vehicle

Pull over safely, stop the vehicle, and stay inside. Avoid overpasses, bridges, and power lines. Once shaking stops, proceed cautiously.

5

After Shaking Stops

Check for injuries and hazards. Be prepared for aftershocks. Turn on a battery-powered radio for emergency information.

6

Evacuate if Necessary

If your building is damaged, evacuate carefully. Use stairs, not elevators. Watch for falling debris and broken glass.
